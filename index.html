Privacy Policy

Forma · Last updated May 2026

The short version

Forma does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. Forma stores your habits and logs locally on your device using iOS standard storage (UserDefaults). This data never leaves your phone and is never sent to any server, analytics platform, or third party.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, location, health data, device identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports, or any other personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no cloud sync.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, they are scheduled entirely on-device using iOS. We have no visibility into whether or when notifications are delivered.

Home screen widget

The Forma widget reads your habit data from a shared local storage area (App Groups) on your device. No data leaves your device as part of widget functionality.

Third-party services

Forma uses no third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or external APIs.

Children

Forma does not knowingly collect any information from children or anyone. Since no data is collected at all, this app is safe for all ages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date at the top.
